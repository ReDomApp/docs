The payment domain is mounted through `/payments` and `/orders-payments`, with refund routes under `/refunds`. The route registry also mounts Stars payment and gifting modules under the orders/payments prefix.

## Payment lifecycle

The backend README describes payment plans, subscriptions, transactions, payment verification, callbacks, webhooks, customer notifications, refunds, and renewal behavior. Transaction records include internal and provider transaction identifiers, user/plan references, amount, currency, purpose, status, checkout URL, gateway status, paid timestamp, metadata, email state and refund lifecycle fields.

## Stripe services

The source service inventory includes Stripe payment handling, saved payment methods, country support and Stars trial services. Payment provider events and transaction state must be verified server-side. A client redirect or browser success page is not authoritative proof of payment.

## ReDom Stars

Stars have separate payment, account, transaction, trial and gifting behavior. The migration history includes Stars payments, creator-economy support, Stripe checkout attempts, failure tracking, trial records and saved payment methods. The inspected trial service credits the trial Stars only after confirming the relevant Stripe setup/payment-method flow and persists the trial state transactionally.

## Refunds and idempotency

Refund routes and services manage review, decision, processing and customer notification. Payment and wallet fulfillment must be idempotent; provider callbacks should be verified and deduplicated before changing transaction or entitlement state.
